# AI Systems Engineering

**Definition:** 
The discipline of designing, implementing, operating, and continuously improving AI-enabled systems across models, data, tools, infrastructure, and people.

**Scope:**
It extends beyond training or prompting individual models to encompass orchestration, knowledge retrieval (RAG), observability, evaluation, latency/cost engineering, and guardrails. It applies systems thinking to probabilistic software.

**Related Terms:**
- **Observability**: The ability to trace prompts, tool calls, latency, token usage, and execution paths in an AI system.
- **Evaluation Pipeline**: Deterministic testing frameworks for probabilistic systems, measuring correctness, hallucination rates, and retrieval quality.
