## Extracted Claims
- AI Systems Engineering is the design, implementation, operation, and improvement of AI-enabled systems across models, data, tools, infrastructure, and people.
- Accuracy is a metric. Reliability is a mindset.
- Production systems require observability, evaluation pipelines, cost monitoring, latency optimization, guardrails, fallbacks, versioning, and human feedback loops.
- The future of AI will be defined by how intelligence is organized, focusing on systems of intelligence, agent orchestration layers, memory architectures, and coordination mechanisms.
