# SEO Style Guide

SEO follows technical usefulness; it does not drive unsupported claims.

- Assign one primary search intent and topic per page.
- Use a literal, specific title and a concise description of the reader outcome.
- Put the answer or decision context near the beginning.
- Use heading hierarchy to expose the conceptual structure.
- Link to prerequisites, parent pillars, and supporting guides with meaningful anchor text.
- Use stable slugs based on the canonical topic, not campaign language.
- Add examples, definitions, and decision criteria only when source-backed.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, duplicate pages, clickbait titles, and fabricated FAQs.

Before publishing, map the page to its pillar, cluster, learning-path position, and at least two internal links where applicable.
