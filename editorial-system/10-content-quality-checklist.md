# Content Quality Checklist

- [ ] The intended audience and outcome are explicit.
- [ ] The page has one primary topic and matches the taxonomy.
- [ ] Claims have evidence, attribution, or an explicit uncertainty label.
- [ ] Prerequisites, constraints, trade-offs, and failure modes are present where relevant.
- [ ] No archive content was silently rewritten or misrepresented.
- [ ] Terms, code, diagrams, and links are internally consistent.
- [ ] The page adds a distinct node rather than duplicating an existing page.
- [ ] Related and next-step links are accurate.
- [ ] Title, headings, and metadata match the actual content.
