# Review Checklist

- [ ] Verify technical claims against cited sources or project evidence.
- [ ] Challenge unstated assumptions and implied causality.
- [ ] Check architecture boundaries, data flow, permissions, and failure handling.
- [ ] Confirm examples are runnable, labelled pseudocode, or removed.
- [ ] Check for leakage of credentials, private data, or unsupported project details.
- [ ] Confirm terminology aligns with the taxonomy and technology map.
- [ ] Verify links, citations, diagrams, metadata, and internal references.
- [ ] Identify what remains unknown and whether it blocks publication.
