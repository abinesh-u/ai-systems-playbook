# Brand Positioning

## Position

AI Systems Playbook is an engineering documentation platform for building reliable AI systems: agentic workflows, context and retrieval systems, model routing, and Production AI Systems.

## Audience

- AI and ML engineers moving from prototypes to production systems.
- Backend and platform engineers adopting agentic AI.
- Architects making trade-offs across models, retrieval, tools, and operations.

## Editorial promise

Explain systems through explicit boundaries, trade-offs, evidence, and operational consequences. Prefer implementation reality over novelty claims.

## Differentiators

- Systems thinking across models, data, tools, infrastructure, and operations.
- Architecture-first framing: responsibilities before products.
- Source-backed technical guidance; uncertainty is stated, not hidden.
- A bridge from learning concepts to production decisions.

## Scope

Primary: AI systems engineering, agentic architecture, RAG, context engineering, production AI, and engineering practice. Supporting: ML/LLM foundations where they unlock those domains. Exclude generic AI news, unsupported vendor comparisons, and untested claims.
