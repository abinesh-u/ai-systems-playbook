# Publication Checklist

- [ ] Draft passed content-quality and technical review.
- [ ] Frontmatter, slug, title, description, and canonical topic are complete.
- [ ] Source assets and external citations are linked.
- [ ] Parent pillar, related pages, and learning path have been updated.
- [ ] Diagrams and code are accessible and render correctly.
- [ ] No placeholder claims, TODOs, or unverified outcomes remain.
- [ ] Publication surface is selected: website, LinkedIn, Medium, newsletter, or reference.
- [ ] Publication record and follow-up review date are captured.
