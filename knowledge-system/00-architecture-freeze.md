## Governance rules

- Every new article maps to a pillar or hub before drafting.
- Pillars are long-lived, comprehensive entry points; hubs are evolving collections.
- Production AI Systems is the canonical label for evaluation, observability, reliability, safety and guardrails, cost, latency, deployment, and continuous improvement.
- Reference Library is documentation infrastructure, not a learning path.
- New top-level categories require deliberate architecture review.
- Architecture changes are recorded here and reflected in the taxonomy, graph, pillar/hub map, and editorial templates.
