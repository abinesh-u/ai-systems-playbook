Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs):

GAN is a deep learning model that involves two neural networks: the generator and the discriminator. The generator creates synthetic data based on a given dataset, while the discriminator attempts to distinguish between real and generated data.

How GANs Work:

Generator: Takes a vector of random noise (Random Z) as input and generates synthetic data that resembles the training dataset.

Discriminator: Analyzes both real data and synthetic data generated by the generator and classifies them as real or fake, outputting a probability score.

Adversarial Process: The generator improves by trying to fool the discriminator with more realistic data. The discriminator sharpens its ability to spot fake data.

Both networks receive feedback on their performance to enhance their outputs iteratively. This adversarial approach is at the heart of many groundbreaking applications in AI, from creating stunning visuals to advancing research.
